averin — offline verifier

Verify an exported evidence bundle entirely in your browser: no network, no trust in the vendor. Runs the same Rust integrity core as the CLI, compiled to WebAssembly — you can read every line of averin.js.

Pin trust roots (optional) — paste an opts.json of role-disjoint keys

Without pinned keys, a PASS proves internal consistency under the bundle's own key claims, not authenticity. Paste the role-disjoint authority key sets you obtained out of band (broker_authority_keys, resource_authority_keys, tsa_keys, attestation_keys, cosig_approver_keys, revocation_keys, federated_broker_keys — all ed25519pub: strings) to verify authenticity and elevate the Tier-A/Tier-B accountability gates.
